# Risk assessment intake

Ask for these in one message. Record an answer only when the user gives it. Leave the rest missing.

## Scope

1. Which document: a threat and risk assessment (TRA), a security risk management plan (SRMP), or both?
2. What is being assessed: the whole entity, one system, the internet-facing technology estate (Technology Security Risk Management Plan), a facility, or a project?
3. Entity name, subject name, and the highest classification of information it handles.
4. The business objectives the subject supports, and anything out of scope.

## Risk settings

5. The entity's risk matrix: likelihood labels, consequence labels, rating levels and the grid. If there is none, the default 5 by 5 matrix is used and labelled.
6. The risk tolerance the Accountable Authority determined, who determined it and when.
7. Who decides on residual risk for this subject (Accountable Authority, or the Authorising Officer for a technology system).

## Assets

8. The people, information, systems and facilities in scope. Which are critical? Who owns each?

## Threats

9. Sector, products and versions, internet-facing services, and any known threat actors or incidents. These drive the threat intelligence lookups.
10. The current National Terrorism Threat Level and any ASIO reporting the entity holds on espionage, foreign interference or sabotage.

## Controls and plan

11. Existing controls for each asset, and how well they are known to work.
12. The risk steward for each risk or category of risk.
13. For an SRMP: security goals, capability maturity, how risk decisions are shared with affected entities, PSPF implementation strategy, arrangements for PSPF Directions, incident management plan, monitoring arrangements, and who approves the plan.
