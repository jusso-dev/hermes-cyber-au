Infosec Registered Assessors Program (IRAP)

Our Infosec Registered Assessors Program (IRAP) gives Australian Government entities and major organisations access to cyber security professionals who conduct high-quality, independent security assessment services.

IRAP assessors do not accredit, endorse or register systems on our behalf.

IRAP security assessments will generally not cover all security controls outlined in the Information Security Manual. Completion of an assessment does not mean the system is compliant with the tested security controls.
