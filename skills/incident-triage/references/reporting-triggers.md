# Reporting triggers

Check three triggers. Hand off when one is open. Do not notify, submit, or post. This is guidance, not legal advice. If `mcp__aureg__aureg_obligation` returns a deadline, cite that envelope and use it instead of the fallback below.

## Possible eligible data breach

Hand off to `ndb-breach-notification` when personal information may have been accessed, disclosed, or lost, and serious harm has not been ruled out. Record the awareness time in Australia/Sydney.

An entity must take all reasonable steps to complete the assessment within 30 calendar days after the day it became aware of the grounds that caused it to suspect an eligible data breach (Privacy Act 1988 s 26WH(2)). A statement to the Commissioner, and notification to individuals at risk, are as soon as practicable when the breach is eligible (s 26WK(2), s 26WL(3)). This skill does not decide eligibility and does not notify the OAIC.

Source: OAIC Part 4 Notifiable Data Breach scheme, none, retrieved 2026-10-17.

## SOCI asset involvement

Hand off to `soci-reporting` when the organisation may be a responsible entity for a critical infrastructure asset, including when the alert says the asset is SOCI-registered. Pass the awareness time. Compilation C2026C00213 includes s 30BC, "Notification of critical cyber security incidents". This skill does not decide which section applies and does not calculate that clock. Do not submit a report.

Source: Security of Critical Infrastructure Act 2018, C2026C00213, retrieved 2026-10-17.

## Ransomware payment

A reporting business entity that makes a ransomware payment, or becomes aware that one was made, must give the designated Commonwealth body a ransomware payment report within 72 hours (Cyber Security Act 2024 s 27). Who counts as a reporting business entity, and the turnover threshold, are set by the Act and the rules. Do not invent the dollar amount.

If no payment has been made, write that the s 27 clock has not started. Do not propose making a payment. If a payment is made later, hand the time of the payment to the reporting skill.

Source: Cyber Security Act 2024, No. 98 of 2024, retrieved 2026-10-17.
